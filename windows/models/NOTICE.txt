Effects models for the Windows client

selfie_segmenter.onnx, face_detector.onnx and face_landmarks_detector.onnx are converted from
Google MediaPipe models, Copyright Google LLC, licensed under the Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE in
this folder):

  selfie_segmenter.onnx         <- selfie_segmenter.tflite
  face_detector.onnx            <- face_detector.tflite            (inside face_landmarker.task)
  face_landmarks_detector.onnx  <- face_landmarks_detector.tflite  (inside face_landmarker.task)

The sources are the files the Android app ships (android/app/src/main/assets):

  selfie_segmenter.tflite  sha256 191ac9529ae506ee0beefa6b2c945a172dab9d07d1e802a290a4e4038226658b
  face_landmarker.task     sha256 64184e229b263107bc2b804c6625db1341ff2bb731874b0bcc2fe6544e0bc9ff

Changes made: converted from TensorFlow Lite to ONNX (opset 17) with tf2onnx; MediaPipe's custom
Convolution2DTransposeBias op replaced by the equivalent standard ConvTranspose; batch size fixed
at 1; initializers renamed. Weights are unchanged. convert.sh rebuilds the files byte for byte and
checks them against SHA256SUMS; postprocess.py documents each graph change.
